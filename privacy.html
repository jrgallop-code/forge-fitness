Level Up Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Level Up is a workout and nutrition tracker for adults. You can use its core features without an account. This policy explains what stays on your device, what may be sent to our services, and the controls available to you.

Information stored on your device

When you use Level Up locally, workout plans and logs, nutrition entries, weight history, measurements, sleep and water entries, goals, settings, notes and photos are stored on your device. Level Up does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Accounts and cloud backup

If you create or connect an account, we collect your email address, account identifier, optional display name and authentication provider. When cloud backup is enabled, your Level Up app data is transmitted to our backend and retained so you can restore or transfer it. A complete backup can include fitness and nutrition records, settings, notes and locally saved media registered with the backup system.

Account and backup data are linked to your Level Up account and used only to authenticate you, operate backup and restore, protect backup versions, provide support and maintain service security.

Optional analytics

Account-linked analytics are off until you choose to share them. If enabled, Level Up may collect active days, completed-workout details such as program, working-set count and duration, food-logging events, appearance choice, program use and acquisition source. Analytics do not include entered food names or quantities, weight values, measurements or photos. You can turn optional analytics off at any time under More → Account & Cloud.

If you deliberately submit a satisfaction rating or written feedback, that response is sent and linked to your account so we can review it. Submitting feedback is optional and separate from the optional analytics setting.

Food search, barcode and voice features

Food search phrases, food identifiers and barcode numbers may be sent through the Level Up backend to food-data providers such as USDA FoodData Central and FatSecret. The camera image used to scan a barcode is not uploaded. Voice-assisted logging uses the device or browser speech-recognition service to convert speech to text; review the resulting text before saving it.

Photos

Photos selected for meal or progress records are stored locally unless you deliberately enable a backup method that includes them. Level Up does not use photos for advertising or analytics.

Support requests

If you send an in-app support request, we receive the issue type, description, optional reproduction steps, optional reply email, submission time, app URL and basic device/browser information. The form is delivered using FormSubmit. Workout, nutrition, weight and backup contents are not automatically attached.

Service providers

We use service providers only to operate Level Up, including Cloudflare for application infrastructure and database hosting, Apple and Google for authentication, USDA FoodData Central and FatSecret for food information, Google Drive when you explicitly connect it for backup, and FormSubmit for support delivery. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms and our configuration of those services.

Retention and security

Account and cloud-backup information is retained while your account remains active. Operational logs and support correspondence may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to secure, troubleshoot and support the service. We use encrypted network connections and access controls. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and deletion

  • Use Level Up without an account and keep core tracking data local.
  • Enable or disable optional analytics from Account & Cloud.
  • Export or remove records using the controls provided in the app.
  • Delete your account in More → Account & Cloud → Delete Cloud Account.

Deleting your cloud account permanently removes the account, server-side backups, sessions and linked analytics records. For accounts using Sign in with Apple, Level Up also revokes the stored Apple authorization. Data that remains locally on your device can be removed through the app’s data controls or by deleting the app.

Children

Level Up is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy when Level Up’s features or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@leveluphypertrophy.com.
