What you'll share with your coach

Connecting Level Up Coach requires access to your synced, coaching-related information so your coach can create programs and monitor progress:

  • Training: workout plans, training sessions, exercise performance, completed workouts and progress.
  • Body metrics: synced weight history, weight trends, measurements, goals, and available steps and sleep metrics.
  • Nutrition: synced calorie and food logs, nutrition targets, macros and expenditure estimates.

Your coach can: review this permitted information and assign or update training programs. Your existing workout and nutrition history stays in your account.

Your coach cannot: see your password or information that has not been uploaded to Level Up Cloud. You can disconnect your coach to stop future access. Information the coach already viewed, downloaded or recorded might not be retractable.

Important: Sharing all three categories is required to use this coaching relationship. You can decline this invitation instead of agreeing.
