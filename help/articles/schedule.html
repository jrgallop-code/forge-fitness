Use the workout schedule

Keep planned sessions aligned with the days you can train.

How to use it

  1. Select your training days during setup or open the workout schedule.
  2. Assign or review the planned sessions for each day.
  3. Open a scheduled session when you are ready to log it.

How to interpret it

A sustainable schedule helps you accumulate training over many weeks. Missed days can be rescheduled; do not try to make up all missed volume at once.
