Find your way around Level Up

The five main areas keep training and nutrition in one place.

How to use it

  1. Open Dashboard for your daily overview and next workout.
  2. Use Workout to build and log a plan.
  3. Use Progress for weight, lifting, nutrition and cardio trends.
  4. Use Nutrition to log food and set goals.
  5. Use More for settings, backups, tutorials and support.
