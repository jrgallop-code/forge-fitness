Review and share workouts

Past sessions provide context for your next session.

How to use it

  1. Open More → Workout History.
  2. Choose a completed workout to review exercises and sets.
  3. Use the share control when available to send a workout summary.
  4. Check the details before sharing; a link is accessible to people who receive it.
