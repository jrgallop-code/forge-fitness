Set up your profile and preferences

Choose a schedule, training setup, units, and optional nutrition tracking.

How to use it

  1. Start the setup flow and choose your training goal, experience, days per week, and typical session length.
  2. Select commercial gym, minimal equipment, or bodyweight training and any exercises you prefer to avoid.
  3. Choose body-weight, lifting-weight, distance, and measurement units separately.
  4. Enter your profile and decide whether to track nutrition; select an appearance.

How to interpret it

These inputs personalize the starting plan. Treat the initial calorie estimate as a starting point; observed intake and weight change give better individual feedback over time.
