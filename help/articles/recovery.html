Understand the recovery map

See recent muscle-group training at a glance.

How to use it

  1. Open Progress and select the recovery view.
  2. Inspect highlighted muscle groups and their recent session history.
  3. Use the display alongside soreness, performance, sleep, and your planned workload.

How to interpret it

The map is a training-history visualization. It cannot directly measure tissue recovery or decide whether you are ready for a hard session.
