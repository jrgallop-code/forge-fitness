Add iOS Home Screen widgets

How it works

The native iPhone app provides small and medium Home Screen widgets for workout and nutrition snapshots. On the iPhone Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, choose Add Widget, search for Level Up, select a size and add it. Follow the app’s widget setup guide. Widgets may refresh on iOS’s schedule and should not be treated as continuously live. This feature is not available from the browser-based PWA.
