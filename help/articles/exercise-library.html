How to use it

  1. Search the exercise library by name, muscle, or equipment.
  2. Open an exercise to review its form guide and available demonstration.
  3. Use the guide alongside your own comfort and equipment setup.

How to interpret it

An exercise guide is an aid, not an individualized assessment. Use a controlled range of motion and stop if a movement causes pain.
