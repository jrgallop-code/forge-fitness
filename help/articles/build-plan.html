Build and save a workout plan

Start from a coach, a library plan or your own plan.

How to use it

  1. Open Workout and find Build With a Coach.
  2. Choose a coach, Browse Library, or Create Manually.
  3. In the guided builder, select goal, experience, schedule, equipment and priorities.
  4. Review days, exercises, sets and rep ranges, then Save Plan.

What the evidence means

A plan is a starting point. Adjust volume and exercise choice as performance and recovery become clearer.
