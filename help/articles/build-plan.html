Build and save a workout plan

Choose a library routine, Smart Build, a manual plan or an imported routine.

How to use it

  1. Open Workout → Workout Plans.
  2. Browse plans under Explore, or tap + Custom Plan to choose a creation route.
  3. Choose Smart Build for guided setup, Create Manually for your own days and exercises, or Import Routine to bring in a plan.
  4. Review days, exercises, sets and rep ranges, then Save Plan.

See it in Level Up

What the evidence means

A plan is a starting point. Adjust volume and exercise choice as performance and recovery become clearer.
