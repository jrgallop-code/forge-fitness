Read the muscle map and weekly sets

Logged exercises contribute to a view of training coverage.

How to use it

  1. Open Progress → Lifting → Muscles.
  2. Choose a time window and inspect the weekly set totals.
  3. Use the muscle map to spot areas receiving more or less logged work.
  4. Compare with your plan and recovery before changing volume.

What the evidence means

Set counts summarize exposure, not the exact muscle growth or recovery of an individual.
