Find restaurant menu items

Browse restaurant entries when eating away from home.

How to use it

  1. Open Food Logging and choose Restaurant Menus.
  2. Select the restaurant, then search or use calorie and protein filters.
  3. Check whether the item is a full meal, whole pizza, or slice before logging.

How to interpret it

Restaurant portions and recipes vary. Treat published nutrition as an estimate and check the restaurant’s current menu for allergens and ingredient changes.
