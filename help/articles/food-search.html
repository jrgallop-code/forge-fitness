Search foods and scan barcodes

Find food entries, then check the serving and nutrition before saving.

How to use it

  1. Open a meal and choose Add Food.
  2. Search by product name or scan a barcode.
  3. Compare the displayed serving size, calories, protein, carbohydrate, and fat with the package.
  4. Change the quantity or choose another result if an entry looks wrong.

How to interpret it

Food databases can contain duplicate or incomplete records. A barcode identifies a product, but it does not verify that its nutrient data is accurate.
