Turn nutrition tracking on or off

Choose whether the nutrition tools appear in your daily workflow.

How to use it

  1. Choose the nutrition option during onboarding, or open More → Health & Records.
  2. Change the nutrition tracking setting.
  3. Return to the dashboard and confirm the tools match your preference.

How to interpret it

Turning off a feature changes the interface; check your backup before clearing any data.
