Use progress photos

Record visual changes with comparable photos.

How to use it

  1. Open Progress Photos and add a photo with its date.
  2. Use similar lighting, distance, pose, and clothing for future photos.
  3. Review photos alongside weight and measurements rather than drawing conclusions from one image.

How to interpret it

Lighting and posture can change apparent physique. Photos are a subjective progress record, not a body-fat measurement.
