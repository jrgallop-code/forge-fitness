Read nutrition charts

Compare calorie intake, expenditure, and other logged nutrition patterns.

How to use it

  1. Open Progress → Nutrition.
  2. Choose a chart and its available date window.
  3. Compare logged calories with the expenditure estimate and inspect the dates behind a change.

How to interpret it

A gap between intake and expenditure is an estimate, not a precise daily fat-gain or fat-loss result. Missing food entries weaken interpretation.
