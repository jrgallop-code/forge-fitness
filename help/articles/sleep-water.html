Log sleep and water

Keep simple records of sleep and hydration.

How to use it

  1. Open the sleep or water tracker from the app.
  2. Enter the duration or water amount for the chosen day.
  3. Review the history and adjust entries if needed.

How to interpret it

These logs offer context for how you feel and train. The app does not diagnose sleep or hydration problems.
