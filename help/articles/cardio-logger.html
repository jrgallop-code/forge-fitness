Log cardio and use its timer

Track cardio sessions and timed intervals.

How to use it

  1. Add a cardio activity to a session and enter the relevant duration or distance.
  2. Start the in-session cardio timer when needed.
  3. Save the activity and view trends in Progress → Cardio.

How to interpret it

Cardio and resistance training can both contribute to health. Match the activity to your goals and build up gradually.
