Set calories and macros

Your plan combines a body profile, goal and macro preference.

How to use it

  1. Open Nutrition → Goals & Plan.
  2. Enter your Body Profile and calculate an initial energy estimate.
  3. Choose a goal in Goals & Calories and save it.
  4. Choose Protein & Macros, inspect gram targets and save.

What the evidence means

Energy estimates are starting points. Actual weight trends and adherence help evaluate whether a target is suitable.
